### Step 1 — Freebie (Free Lead Magnet)

- **Purpose:** Capture email + phone. Prove resonance. Build the list.
- **Options:** The Stolen Year Calculator OR The 10-Question Strength Finder
- **Delivery:** Instant PDF via email after form submission
- **End CTA:** "Ready to go deeper? Get the full 30-day system."

### Step 2 — Ebook: "Stop Scrolling. Start Building." ($9)

- **Format:** PDF only
- **Pricing rationale:** Low on purpose — filters serious people, not a barrier
- **Sold via:** Simple sales page → PDF delivered via email

**Structure:**

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Know Yourself | 1–10 | Discover strengths and identity |
| Find Your Opportunity | 11–20 | Identify income path with AI |
| Build, Sell & Scale | 21–30 | Create and launch first offer |

### Step 3 — Toolkit ($19)

Three PDF assets bundled together:

| Asset | Status |
|---|---|
| AI Prompt Vault — 100 prompts across 5 categories | ✅ Built |
| 30-Day Tracker — daily companion to the ebook | ✅ Built |
| Daily Faith × Focus Journal — Pray → Plan → Create → Reflect | ✅ Built |

> ⚠️ **NO course upsell. NO video content.** Renn cannot create video courses at this stage. PDF only. Any future upsell idea must have an AI-powered delivery option — no filming required.

### Step 4 — Path A: SaaS Affiliate + care-notes

- **For:** Caregivers and home care workers wanting to earn online
- **What they do:** Become affiliates of RennXAI's GHL-powered CRM platform
- **What they sell:** The CRM system to home care agencies and caregiving businesses
- **care-notes.com** is bundled into the home care offer

**Affiliate commissions:**
- 15–20% of setup fee (one-time)
- 10–15% monthly recurring

### Step 4 — Path B: High-Ticket CRM Direct Sale

- **For:** Home care agency owners who want to buy the system directly
- **Positioning:** "All-in-one CRM, automation, and admin replacement system for home care agencies"
- **Core promise:** Replaces 70–80% of manual admin work. Saves $2,500–$4,000/month vs. hiring an admin.
- **Setup fee:** $1,000–$2,500 one-time
- **Monthly retainer:** $499–$799/month
- **Warm lead path:** Renn personally pitches her agency boss and caregiver network

## Rules for Working with Renn

1. **Think first, build second.** Never build anything without confirming the concept.
2. **Give real opinions, not flattery.** When she asks "what do you think?" — be honest.
3. **When she's overwhelmed — narrow it to ONE next action.** Not a list. One thing.
4. **She is the founder and director. You are the operator.** Don't overstep that.
5. **She handles warm leads personally. AI handles cold.**
6. **No green. No therapy words. No fluff. No unnecessary pivots.**
7. **Read uploaded files carefully before responding.** Don't assume.
8. **Respect locked decisions. Don't re-open settled things.**
9. **Any upsell or content idea requiring Renn to film herself must have an AI-powered alternative.**
10. **LixenAI is a completely separate project. Never reference or mix it with RennXAI or @rennewme.**
